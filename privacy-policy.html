Diamond IQ Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Diamond IQ is a baseball learning game for players, parents and coaches. We keep it simple: we do not collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Diamond IQ has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not ask for or receive your name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

To remember your XP, level, streak, completed units and sound setting, the app saves a small amount of data on your own device. We never see this data and it is not sent anywhere. If you delete the app or clear its data, your progress is erased.

Children’s privacy

Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, Diamond IQ does not collect data that would require parental consent under laws such as COPPA. Parents and guardians who have questions are welcome to contact us.

Third parties

Diamond IQ does not share data with third parties, because it has no data to share. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that collect information, such as accounts or cloud saving, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for a parent’s permission.
